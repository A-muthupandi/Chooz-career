Career Roadmap: Business Management

Core Overview: Leads business operations, manages corporate budgets, orchestrates team dynamics, and scales startup or enterprise ventures.

Core Skillsets

1) Operations

Strategic Planning, Project Management, Supply Chain

2) Finance

Corporate Budgeting, Financial Analysis, ROI Optimization

3) Leadership

Team Building, Conflict Resolution, Human Resources

4) Soft Skills

Negotiation, Critical Thinking, Public Speaking

Career Progression Tiers

Management Trainee / Associate
→
Operations Manager
→
Operations Director / COO

Execution Learning Roadmap

1) Business Fundamentals & Administration

Master microeconomics, organizational behavior, and foundational accounting principles.

2) Tactical Execution & Resource Optimization

Learn risk management models, execute cross-functional workflows, and analyze market trends.

3) Strategic Leadership & Enterprise Scaling

Formulate corporate expansion blueprints, oversee large mergers, and manage enterprise P&L matrices.
