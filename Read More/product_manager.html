Career Roadmap: Product Manager

Core Overview: Defines product vision, prioritizes feature roadmaps, and aligns cross-functional engineering, design, and business teams.

Core Skillsets

1) Strategy

Product Vision, Market Research, Roadmapping

2) Execution

Agile, Scrum, PRD Writing, Backlog Grooming

3) Analytics

KPIs, A/B Testing, SQL, Funnel Metrics

4) Soft Skills

Stakeholder Alignment, Customer Discovery

Career Progression Tiers

Associate Product Manager
→
Senior Product Manager
→
VP of Product / CPO

Execution Learning Roadmap

1) Customer Discovery & Agile Fundamentals

Master user interviews, problem framing, Scrum workflows, and writing PRDs.

2) Prioritization Frameworks & Product Metrics

Apply RICE scoring, define North Star metrics, and ship Minimum Viable Products (MVPs).

3) Go-To-Market Strategy & Portfolio Scaling

Lead cross-functional launches, optimize user retention, and manage multi-quarter roadmaps.
